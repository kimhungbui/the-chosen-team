# Request for Proposal — Omnichannel Personalization & Recommendation Engine

**Client:** Apex Commerce Ltd. (fictional)
**Industry:** Retail & E-Commerce

## 1. Background & Business Context
Apex Commerce operates an omnichannel retail footprint across online e-commerce storefronts, mobile applications, and 45 physical retail locations. To increase customer lifetime value and basket size, we require an intelligent, AI-powered personalization and recommendation engine that delivers dynamic product suggestions.

## 2. Customer Requirements
1. **Real-Time Recommendation Engine:** The platform must deliver blazing fast performance across all web, mobile, and in-store kiosk touchpoints with zero lag.
2. **System Integration:** Must seamlessly integrate with our existing tools and legacy retail database infrastructure without disruption.
3. **Delivery Timeline:** Go-live should occur as soon as practical to capture upcoming seasonal promotional traffic.
4. **Commercial Terms:** Pricing must be reasonable and cost-effective, offering flexible terms suitable for an enterprise pilot.
5. **Security & Data Governance:** The vendor must provide enterprise-grade security and standard data protection across all consumer profile interactions.
